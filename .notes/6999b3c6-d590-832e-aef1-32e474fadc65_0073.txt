3. What This Likely Means Architecturally

There are three plausible architectural issues:
A. Cancellation Pathway Misuse

cancelToolRun() or an AbortController may be calling close(true).

If so:

    Request-scoped cancellation is incorrectly escalated to global shutdown.

    That violates separation of concerns between:

        request lifecycle

        transport lifecycle

B. Background Stream Abort Coupling

If remote-agents/list-stream times out:

    It may share cancellation plumbing with tool execution.

    Its abort may cascade into MCP client shutdown.

This would explain:

    Recurring ~60 second pattern

    Worsening after update

    No direct user cancellation required

C. Missing Reset After Graceful Close

Even if shutdown is intentional:

    _closingPromise should be reset after completion.

    Or a new client should be reinitialized automatically.

Without reset, shutdown becomes permanent.
